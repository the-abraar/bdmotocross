Facility Communication

Brand Voice: Welcoming, Clear & Safety-Disciplined

Paddock communication balancing family hospitality with strict track marshaling.

✅ Mandatory Facility Voice
  • Utility & Clarity First: Give riders exact times, track moisture conditions, grooming reports, and gate fees upfront.
  • Youth Development Welcoming: Encourage young riders and parents, emphasizing personal growth and mechanical discipline.
  • Strict Track Marshaling: Enforce yellow flag safety holds and zero tolerance for dangerous riding in the paddock area.
❌ Strictly Forbidden
  • No Vague Marketing Promises: Never hide practice hours, waiver requirements, or gear rules behind marketing slogans.
  • No Street Riding Association: Always emphasize that BMCA is an off-highway, closed-course recreational facility.
