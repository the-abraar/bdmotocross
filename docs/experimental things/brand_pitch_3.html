Brand Voice: High-Voltage & Fearless

Action sports communication parameters.

✅ Mandatory Kinetic Voice
  • High Energy & Direct: Speak with the kinetic enthusiasm of extreme sports broadcasts and stadium announcers.
  • Focus on Speed & Mastery: Emphasize throttle control, balance, and athletic agility.
  • Safety as Empowerment: Position armor as the gear that unleashes your true riding potential.
❌ Strictly Forbidden
  • Never Generic Corporate: No stiff corporate press release language.
  • No Street Stunting: Keep all action strictly framed on the closed dirt arena.
  • No Over-the-Top Slang: Maintain sports credibility while being energetic.
