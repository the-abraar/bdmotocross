Curriculum Architecture

The 4-Tier Color-Coded Track Progression

Modeled directly after the MX-Academy Swiss training circuit layout.

Stage 01 • Green Track

Novice & Junior Track

Honda CRF110F / CRF125F (Gov Limited)

Flat surface training focused exclusively on clutch friction zone, smooth braking, standing peg posture, and line vision.

Stage 02 • Blue Track

Intermediate Berm Circuit

Honda CRF150RB / CRF250F

Banked loam berms, small tabletop roll-overs, acceleration out of corners, and body weight transfer.

Stage 03 • Red Track

Advanced GP Circuit

Honda CRF250R Competition

Tabletop jumps, braking bumps, whoop sections, passing lines, and competitive race endurance pacing.

Stage 04 • Black Track

Pro Championship Proving Ground

Honda CRF250R / CRF450R Pro

International gate drops, deep rut navigation, elevation drop jumps, and telemetry data telemetry analysis.
