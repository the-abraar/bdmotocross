Pioneering Bangladesh Motorsports

Building The Motocross Community From Chittagong

Operating from the coastal hills and challenging terrain of Chittagong, Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy (BMCA) brings together passionate riders, mechanics, off-road enthusiasts, and national competitors into a unified championship ecosystem.

Whether preparing for high-octane stadium trials or navigating rugged hill tracks across Chittagong, Feni, Rajshahi, and Jessore, our club provides structured training, racing opportunities, and community support.

Track Safety First
Mandatory full body armor, ECE/DOT helmets, and strict marshaled tracks.
National & Global Vision
International mentor credentials and cross-border race participation.

Skill Progression

Train With Purpose & Safety

The BMCA Academy training curriculum is engineered for all skill tiers—from introductory clutch and balance mastery for aspiring youngsters to high-speed racecraft and rut-railing for seasoned competitors.

⚡ Enroll in Academy 📞 Call Coach
01

Bike Control & Body Stance

Throttle modulation, clutch biting point, standing pegs posture, and center of gravity balance.

02

Cornering, Berms & Rut Traversal

Leg-out positioning, counter-steering, apex braking, and locking into deep dirt ruts.

03

Jumping & Suspension Pre-load

Approach speed, flight trajectory control, tabletop absorption, and smooth landing throttle.

04

Gate Starts & Racecraft

Starting gate reaction, holeshot execution, track lines, defensive passing, and safety flags.

🛡️ Protective Armor & Gear 🏁 Track Riding & Flag Signals 🔧 Dirt Bike Mechanical Maintenance ⚡ Competition Gate Drops 🏔️ Hill Track & Enduro Navigation 🧠 Mental Discipline & Conditioning
