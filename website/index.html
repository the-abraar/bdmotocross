Pioneering Bangladesh Motorsports

Building The Motocross Community From Chittagong

Operating from the rugged hills and challenging coastal terrain of Chittagong, Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy (BMCA) unifies active riders, factory tuners, off-road enthusiasts, and national championship contenders.

Whether competing in regional track trials or navigating demanding hill trails across Chittagong, Feni, Rajshahi, and Jessore, BMCA delivers structured athletic coaching, certified track marshaling, and sanctioned race entries.

Track Safety Protocol
Mandatory DOT/ECE full-face helmets, chest/roost armor, and trained track marshals.
Certified Coaching Credentials
Mentors hold international certification from TWM Academy of Moto Adventure (Kolkata, India).
🏍️
Chittagong Dirt Arena Sanctioned Trials & Academy Track
🏁

Sanctioned Racing Circuits

Natural loam and compact red clay tracks featuring elevation drops, banked berms, tabletop jumps, and rhythm sections engineered for 150cc–450cc competition.

🎯

Dedicated Training Fleet

Students have access to BMCA's fleet of maintained training bikes, or may bring their personal motorcycles for trackside suspension sag tuning and gearing optimization.

🛡️

Strict Trackside Marshaling

Zero-tolerance safety standard: flag marshals (Yellow, Red, Chequered) at every blind corner, mandatory full body armor, and on-site first-aid medical support.

Academy Prerequisites & Fleet Logistics

Essential Information Before Enrolling

Practical guidelines answering key questions on motorcycle availability, armor requirements, age tiers, and track conditions.

🏍️
Bike Fleet & Ownership

Academy Fleet Available BMCA maintains 150cc–250cc dirt bikes for enrolled trainees. Trainees owning personal off-road or dual-sport motorcycles are encouraged to bring them for personalized setup.

🛡️
Mandatory Safety Armor

DOT / ECE Certified Full-face helmet, goggles, chest/roost protector, knee guards, and rigid motocross boots are compulsory. Sanitized loaner safety sets are available for trial sessions.

👥
Age & Experience Levels

Ages 12+ to Adult Pro Junior Division (12–17 years with parental consent) and Senior Division (18+). Prior off-road experience is not required; basic manual clutch familiarity is beneficial.

📍
Chittagong Dirt Arena

Natural Loam & Clay Circuit Challenging topography with elevation climbs, berms, rhythm whoops, and safety flag marshals. Paddock shade and trackside mechanical pits are provided.

Skill Progression

Train With Purpose & Precision

Under the direct oversight of certified mentor Salauddin Ahmed Bulbul (trained at Kolkata's TWM Academy), our curriculum progresses riders from fundamental clutch control to high-speed racecraft.

Enroll in Academy Call Academy Coach
01

Bike Control & Body Stance

Throttle modulation, clutch biting point, standing pegs posture, and center of gravity balance.

02

Cornering, Berms & Rut Traversal

Leg-out positioning, counter-steering, apex braking, and locking into deep dirt ruts.

03

Jumping & Suspension Pre-load

Approach speed, flight trajectory control, tabletop absorption, and smooth landing throttle.

04

Gate Starts & Racecraft

Starting gate reaction, holeshot execution, track lines, defensive passing, and safety flags.
