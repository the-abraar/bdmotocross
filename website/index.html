01 The club

Building the motocross community from the hills of Chittagong — and racing it to every corner of Bangladesh.

Operating from the rugged hills and challenging coastal terrain of Chittagong, Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy unifies active riders, factory tuners, off-road enthusiasts and national championship contenders.

Whether competing in regional track trials or navigating demanding hill trails across Chittagong, Feni, Rajshahi and Jessore, BMCA delivers structured athletic coaching, certified track marshaling and sanctioned race entries.

  • Track safety protocolMandatory DOT/ECE full-face helmets, chest/roost armor and trained track marshals.
  • Certified coachingMentors hold international certification from TWM Academy of Moto Adventure, Kolkata.
  1. 01

    Sanctioned racing circuits

    Natural loam and compact red clay tracks with elevation drops, banked berms, tabletop jumps and rhythm sections built for 150cc–450cc competition.

  2. 02

    Dedicated training fleet

    Students ride BMCA's maintained training bikes, or bring their own for trackside suspension sag tuning and gearing optimisation.

  3. 03

    Strict trackside marshaling

    Zero-tolerance safety: yellow, red and chequered flag marshals at every blind corner, mandatory body armor and on-site first aid.

04 The academy

Train with
purpose.

Under the direct oversight of certified mentor Salauddin Ahmed Bulbul (trained at Kolkata's TWM Academy), the curriculum takes riders from fundamental clutch control to high-speed racecraft.

Stage
01/04

  1. Bike control & body stance

    Throttle modulation, clutch biting point, standing-on-the-pegs posture and centre-of-gravity balance.

  2. Cornering, berms & ruts

    Leg-out positioning, counter-steering, apex braking and locking into deep dirt ruts.

  3. Jumping & suspension pre-load

    Approach speed, flight trajectory control, tabletop absorption and smooth landing throttle.

  4. Gate starts & racecraft

    Starting-gate reaction, holeshot execution, track lines, defensive passing and safety flags.

  • Protective armor & gear
  • Track riding & flag signals
  • Dirt bike maintenance
  • Competition gate drops
  • Hill track & enduro navigation
  • Mental discipline & conditioning

Before you enroll

Bike fleetAcademy fleet available

BMCA maintains 150cc–250cc dirt bikes for enrolled trainees. Own an off-road or dual-sport bike? Bring it for personalised setup.

Safety armorDOT / ECE certified

Full-face helmet, goggles, chest/roost protector, knee guards and rigid MX boots are compulsory. Sanitised loaner sets for trial sessions.

Age & level12+ to adult pro

Junior Division (12–17, with parental consent) and Senior Division (18+). No off-road experience needed; manual clutch familiarity helps.

Home trackChittagong Dirt Arena

Natural loam & clay with elevation climbs, berms and rhythm whoops. Flag marshals, paddock shade and trackside mechanical pits.

06 Ground zero for sanctioned motorsport

Leave the stunts off the streets. Bring your courage to the dirt.

A disciplined, athletic and safe alternative to reckless street stunting: closed, marshaled circuits, certified armor, regulated machines and medical standby.

Zero-compromise
safety.

For parents and guardians: here is how BMCA manages risk and keeps every training session in Chittagong in line with international motorsport safety protocol.

Armor protocol

Mandatory personal armor

No unarmored riding, ever. Certified DOT/ECE full-face helmet, shatterproof goggles, chest & roost protector, knee armor and rigid boots. No clearance, no staging gate.

Marshal discipline

Track marshal flag rules

  • Yellow incident zone — slow down, no overtaking
  • Red full track emergency halt
  • Chequered controlled session finish
Equipment control

Low-speed throttle stops

Junior Division riders (12–17) ride machines with adjustable throttle limiters. Power unlocks step by step as balance, clutch discipline and emergency stopping are certified.

Emergency ready

On-site first-aid standby

Every training weekend runs a paddock first-aid post — trauma splints, neck stabilisation, burn dressings and evacuation routing to Chittagong medical centres.
