Md. Sultan Ahmed

Pro Category Racer • Sultan Sheikh (#77)

Md. Sultan Ahmed (popularly known as Sultan Sheikh #77) is an elite Pro Category competitor representing Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy. Originating from Sylhet, his fearless riding style on technical dirt slopes and steep hill trails has established him as a premier competitor in national off-road trials.
