Official Profile

Md. Mahabubur Rahman Hafiz

Director • Champion Racer

Md. Mahabubur Rahman Hafiz, widely recognized across Bangladeshi motorsports as MR99, is the founding Director of Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy and an acclaimed pro track champion. Based in Chittagong, he has dedicated his career to building competitive tracks, organizing safety protocols, and coaching the next generation of riders.
